## 1. Publishing Status — Billing Limitation

**Status:** Agent publish action blocked by tenant billing issue.

During final submission, clicking **Publish** in Copilot Studio displayed the following error:
> *"There is a billing issue. Please contact your admin to confirm the billing capability for this environment and agent."*

This is a tenant-level Microsoft Power Platform billing restriction on the current environment and is outside the participant's control.
