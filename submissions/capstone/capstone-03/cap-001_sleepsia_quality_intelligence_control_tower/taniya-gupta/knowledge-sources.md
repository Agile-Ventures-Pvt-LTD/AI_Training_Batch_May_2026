# Knowledge Sources & Precedence

## 1. Configured Knowledge Sources

| Source Name | Type | File / URL | Authority / Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Sleepsia Product Quality Policy** | Word Document | `Sleepsia_Product_Quality_Policy.docx` | **HIGHEST** internal authority for quality thresholds, classification, CAPA, and escalation rules. |
| **Sleepsia Product Care & Usage Guide** | Word Document | `Sleepsia_Product_Care_and_Usage_Guide.docx` | Product care, washing instructions, material specifications, and safety boundary limits. |
| **Sleepsia Customer Resolution Policy** | Word Document | `Sleepsia_Customer_Resolution_Policy.docx` | Customer resolution vs quality investigation ownership boundaries. |
| **Sleepsia Travel Pillow Page** | Public Web URL | `https://www.sleepsia.in/products/travel-pillow` | Public product facts only (lower than internal policy). |
| **Sleepsia Kids Alpha Pillow Page** | Public Web URL | `https://www.sleepsia.in/products/kids-alpha-pillow` | Public product facts only (lower than internal policy). |

---

## 2. Knowledge Precedence Rule
Internal quality severity and escalation rules are strictly governed by synthetic internal policy documents (`Sleepsia_Product_Quality_Policy.docx`). Public product marketing URLs provide supplementary product specifications only and cannot override internal quality threshold decisions.
