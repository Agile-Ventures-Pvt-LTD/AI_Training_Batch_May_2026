# Known Limitations

## 1. Tenant and Licensing
Teams and Microsoft 365 Copilot availability depends on organizational permissions, licensing, sharing settings and app policies.

## 2. Microsoft Learn MCP
Microsoft Learn MCP is operational guidance only. Its unavailability must not block the core Sleepsia quality assessment.

## 3. Tool Failures
Excel, Word and Outlook failures must be represented explicitly. The system must never claim that a failed action succeeded.

## 4. Evidence Availability
Missing or incomplete evidence may result in Insufficient Evidence or Manual Review. The system must not manufacture missing values.

## 5. Reassessment
Selective reassessment is bounded to prevent indefinite autonomous loops. Repeated unresolved reassessment must move to Manual Review.

## 6. Public Product Sources
Approved public product URLs provide product facts only. They do not override internal quality policies.

## 7. Data Scope
The solution should use the supplied synthetic/project data. Real customer PII, payment information, medical records or unrelated confidential information should not be introduced.

## 8. Medical Advice
The agent does not provide medical diagnosis or treatment advice.

## 9. Testing
The final operational limitations discovered during real test execution must be appended to this document after testing.

## 10. Channel Availability
If a required publishing channel cannot be enabled because of tenant constraints, the limitation should be documented with evidence rather than bypassed.
