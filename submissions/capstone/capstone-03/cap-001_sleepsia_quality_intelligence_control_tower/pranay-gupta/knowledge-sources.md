# Knowledge Sources

## 1. Supplied Documents

### Sleepsia_Product_Quality_Policy.docx
Primary internal authority for quality thresholds, severity/classification rules, investigation, escalation and CAPA decisions.

### Sleepsia_Product_Care_and_Usage_Guide.docx
Used for product-care and usage information and related approved guidance. It does not override the internal quality policy.

### Sleepsia_Customer_Resolution_Policy.docx
Used for customer-resolution boundaries and escalation ownership. It does not replace the internal quality policy for quality classification.

## 2. Approved Public Product Sources
- https://www.sleepsia.in/products/travel-pillow
- https://www.sleepsia.in/products/kids-alpha-pillow

These sources are for product facts and public product information.

## 3. Knowledge Precedence
For internal quality decisions, the supplied internal quality policy is authoritative.

For product-care questions, use the supplied care and usage guidance.

For customer-resolution questions, use the customer-resolution policy.

For public product facts, use the approved Sleepsia product pages.

A public product statement must never override an internal quality threshold, severity rule, escalation rule or CAPA rule.

## 4. Retrieval Boundary
Knowledge retrieval should remain grounded in the approved sources. If the required evidence is unavailable, the agent should state the limitation rather than inventing information.

## 5. Sensitive Information
The implementation should use only the supplied synthetic/project data and should not introduce real customer PII, payment data or medical records.
