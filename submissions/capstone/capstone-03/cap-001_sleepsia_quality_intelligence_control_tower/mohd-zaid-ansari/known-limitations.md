## Overview

The solution is implemented using Microsoft Copilot Studio and Microsoft 365 services. The following limitations are based on the current tenant, platform, and connector capabilities.

---

## Platform Limitations

- Requires Microsoft Copilot Studio licensing and appropriate permissions.
- Performance depends on Microsoft 365 service availability.
- Connector execution is subject to Microsoft service limits.

---

## Excel Limitations

- Concurrent updates may cause row conflicts.
- Large datasets can increase response time.
- Data accuracy depends on the maintained Excel tables.

---

## Microsoft Word & Outlook

- Report generation depends on Word Online availability.
- Email delivery depends on Outlook service configuration and permissions.

---

## Knowledge Sources

- Responses are limited to the configured Sleepsia documents and approved public URLs.
- Missing or outdated knowledge sources may affect response completeness.

---

## Microsoft Learn MCP

- Available only when the MCP server is accessible.
- Used exclusively for Microsoft platform guidance and not for business decisions.

---

## Operational Limitations

- Investigation quality depends on accurate input data.
- Invalid or incomplete complaint information may stop the workflow.
- Manual review may be required for exceptional or unsupported scenarios.

---

## Conclusion

Despite these limitations, the implemented solution provides a reliable, governed, and scalable quality investigation workflow that satisfies the project requirements.
