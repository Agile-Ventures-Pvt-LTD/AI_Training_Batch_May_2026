# Knowledge Sources

## Internal Sources
1. `Sleepsia_Product_Quality_Policy.docx` — primary authority for quality thresholds, investigation, classification, escalation and CAPA.
2. `Sleepsia_Product_Care_and_Usage_Guide.docx` — product care/use guidance.
3. `Sleepsia_Customer_Resolution_Policy.docx` — customer-resolution responsibilities and boundaries.

## Public Source
Approved Sleepsia public product URL(s) may provide public product facts but must not override internal quality policy.

## Precedence
Internal quality policy > care/use guide for care/use > customer-resolution policy for resolution boundaries > approved public product source for public facts.

## Retrieval Validation
Test that threshold, care/use, customer-resolution and public-product questions retrieve the correct source.

## Safety
Do not diagnose medical conditions or provide medical advice.
