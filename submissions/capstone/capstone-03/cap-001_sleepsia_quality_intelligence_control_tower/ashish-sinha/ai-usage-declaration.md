# AI Usage Declaration

## Project
CAP-001 — Sleepsia Product Quality & Customer Experience Intelligence Control Tower

## AI Tools
- Microsoft Copilot Studio
- Copilot Studio generative AI capabilities where enabled
- ChatGPT as an implementation/documentation assistant

## AI-Assisted Work
AI assistance was used for agent-instruction drafting/refinement, orchestration/topic planning, documentation, test organization and implementation guidance.

## Human Validation
The project participant must validate final Copilot Studio configuration, connectors, tool execution, MCP behavior, knowledge sources, decision rules, test results and screenshots.

AI-generated text is not evidence of successful tool execution.

## Quality/Safety
Missing operational evidence must never be replaced by AI assumptions. Quality decisions follow the approved internal policy and deterministic workflow.
