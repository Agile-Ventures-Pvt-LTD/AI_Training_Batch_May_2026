# Known Limitations

## Tenant / Publishing
Teams and Microsoft 365 Copilot publishing may depend on tenant permissions, licensing and availability.

## Connector Permissions
Excel Online (Business), Word Online (Business) and Outlook require valid connections and permissions.

## MCP
Microsoft Learn MCP availability may vary by tenant/environment. MCP failure must not block core quality processing.

## Orchestration
Logical fan-out/fan-in is required; literal simultaneous execution is not required.

## Evidence
Missing evidence must result in an evidence/review path, never fabricated values.
