# AI Usage Declaration

**Project Name:** CAP-001 | Sleepsia Product Quality & Customer Experience Intelligence Control Tower  
**Platform:** Microsoft Copilot Studio  
**Date:** 10 August 2026  

---

## 1. Primary AI Tool Used
* **AI Platform:** ChatGPT (OpenAI) & Antigravity

---

## 2. Declared AI Usage Scope

ChatGPT (OpenAI) was utilized strictly as an assistant for the following activities during project development:

1. **Prompt Engineering & Topic Logic Structuring:**
   - Assisting in drafting and refining Power Fx expressions and YAML dialog logic for mandatory custom topics (intake validation, decision precedence, CAPA planning, and reassessment loops).

2. **Summarization & Data Extraction:**
   - Summarizing internal policy documentation (`Sleepsia_Product_Quality_Policy.docx`) into deterministic decision rules.
   - Synthesizing specialist agent output schemas and notification summaries.

3. **Test Case Dataset Generation:**
   - Generating, formatting, and structuring the test scenario matrix and execution logs into standard `.csv` file formats for automated and manual verification (TC-01 through TC-20).

---

## 3. Human Oversight & Validation

- **Validation Responsibility:** All AI-assisted prompts, formulas, Adaptive Card JSON templates, and generated test scenarios were manually reviewed, verified, and tested within Microsoft Copilot Studio.
- **Compliance:** No synthetic policy rules, public product facts, or incident decisions were hallucinated or accepted without direct verification against the official CAP-001 PRD specifications.
