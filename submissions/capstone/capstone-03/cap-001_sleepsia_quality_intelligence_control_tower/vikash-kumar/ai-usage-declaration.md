# 🤖 AI Usage Declaration
## Platform
Microsoft Copilot Studio is the primary AI development platform.
The solution uses AI agents for quality investigation.
The Supervisor coordinates specialist agents.
Generative AI supports interpretation and response generation.

## AI Components
The Supervisor is an AI orchestration component.
The Product / Batch Specialist is an AI investigation component.
The Complaint Pattern Specialist is an AI analysis component.
The Returns Specialist is an AI investigation component.
The Customer Impact Specialist is an AI analysis component.
The Safety Specialist is an AI evidence component.
The CAPA Specialist is an AI planning component.

## AI Responsibilities
AI can interpret user requests.
AI can identify the relevant workflow.
AI can retrieve relevant information through configured tools.
AI can analyze complaint evidence.
AI can summarize specialist findings.
AI can support CAPA planning.
AI can generate structured business outputs.

## Human and Supervisor Governance
The Supervisor owns the final quality decision.
Specialists provide findings.
Operational actions should be controlled by configured workflow logic.
AI should not invent missing information.
AI should not override configured governance.

## Evidence Policy
Observed data must be distinguished from generated interpretation.
Calculated findings should be based on available records.
Missing information should be reported.
Unsupported assumptions should be avoided.
Insufficient Evidence should be returned when required evidence is unavailable.

## Safety
The Safety Specialist identifies safety evidence.
The Safety Specialist does not provide medical diagnosis.
The Safety Specialist does not provide treatment advice.
The Supervisor owns final safety-related routing.
Safety evidence should be grounded in available records.

## Validation
AI outputs should be tested.
Tool results should be validated.
Specialist boundaries should be tested.
Conditional routing should be tested.
Reassessment behavior should be tested.
Final operational outputs should be checked.

## Transparency
AI is used as part of the investigation workflow.
AI findings are not automatically treated as independent truth.
Source evidence remains important.
The Supervisor provides governance over the agentic process.
