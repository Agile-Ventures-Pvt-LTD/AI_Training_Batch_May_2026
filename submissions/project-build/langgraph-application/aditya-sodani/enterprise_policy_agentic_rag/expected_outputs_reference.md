# Expected Outputs Reference

This trainer/evaluator reference provides expected answer points. Exact wording may vary, but participant answers should be grounded in policy evidence and cite sources.

## Q1
Maximum 6 unused annual leave days can be carried forward. Carry-forward leave must be used by 31 March of the next calendar year.

## Q2
Same-day domestic business travel must be manager-approved. Meal reimbursement is allowed under the reimbursement policy up to INR 800 per day with valid receipts.

## Q3
Hotel reimbursement requires hotel invoice, payment receipt, approved travel request, check-in/check-out dates, and GST details where applicable. It is only for approved overnight business travel unless overnight stay was separately approved.

## Q4
Personal laptops should not be used for regular office work. Temporary use requires manager approval and IT security approval. Confidential or customer data should not be stored locally.

## Q5
International business travel requires reporting manager approval, business unit head approval, finance approval, and travel desk confirmation before ticket booking.

## Q6
Customer data must not be uploaded to public AI tools. Customer data may be used only with an approved internal AI tool after governance approvals and controls.

## Q7
The assistant must not guarantee reimbursement approval. Approval depends on business purpose, prior approval, receipts, claim timeline, policy limits, and Finance review.

## Q8
The assistant should not invent policy. It should recommend contacting the relevant owner: HR for leave, Finance for reimbursements, IT Security for security questions, and AI Governance Committee for AI usage questions.
