# ENTERPRISE POLICY AGENTIC RAG

1. How many annual leave days can an employee carry forward?

An employee can carry forward a maximum of **6 unused annual leave days** into the next calendar year.


2. Can I claim meals for same-day domestic business travel?

Yes, meal reimbursement is allowed for approved business travel, including same-day domestic business travel, up to INR 800 per day, provided valid receipts are submitted.

3. What documents are needed for hotel reimbursement?

Receipts are mandatory for hotel stays.
Receipts must clearly show date, amount, vendor name, and expense description.

4. Can I use my personal laptop for office work?

Employees should not use personal laptops for regular office work. However, there are exceptions when certain conditions are met, such as temporary business need, manager approval, IT security approval, no confidential or customer data stored locally, and the device meeting minimum security requirements.

5. What approvals are needed for international travel?

International business travel requires:
- reporting manager approval
- business unit head approval
- finance approval
- travel desk confirmation before ticket booking.

6. Can customer data be uploaded to a public AI tool?

Confidential information must not be entered into public AI systems, and customer names and contact details are considered confidential information. Therefore, it can be inferred that customer data should not be uploaded to a public AI tool.

7. Will my reimbursement definitely be approved?

Reimbursement Policy - Reimbursement approval is not guaranteed. Finance may reject claims that do not meet policy conditions.
