# Evaluation Prompts
## Report Relevance
Check whether:
1The screening report is relevant to the AI Engineer JD.
2 Candidate strengths align with resume content.
3 Candidate gaps align with JD requirements.
---
## Groundedness
Ensure:
1 No skills are invented.
2 No projects are invented.
3 No certifications are invented.
4 All evidence exists in JD or Resume.
---
## Recommendation Consistency
Verify recommendation matches score:
1 80–100 = STRONG_MATCH
2 60–79 = MODERATE_MATCH
3 40–59 = WEAK_MATCH
4 Below 40 = NEEDS_MANUAL_REVIEW
---
## Interview Quality
Validate:
- Technical questions are role relevant.
- Scenario questions are relevant.
- Gap validation questions address missing skills.
- Questions are candidate specific.
