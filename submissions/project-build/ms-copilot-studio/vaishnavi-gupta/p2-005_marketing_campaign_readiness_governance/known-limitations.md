# Known Limitations

1. The solution uses synthetic campaign data and is not intended for production use.
2. Final campaign readiness depends on the accuracy and availability of Excel data and configured knowledge sources.
3. Human approval is still required for governance conditions that require management or regional approval.
4. Specialist failures may require manual review after the permitted retry.
5. Automated reassessment is limited to two cycles.
6. Word report or Outlook failures may require manual follow-up.
7. The system does not autonomously launch or publish marketing campaigns.
8. Connector availability and permissions may vary by the Copilot Studio environment.
