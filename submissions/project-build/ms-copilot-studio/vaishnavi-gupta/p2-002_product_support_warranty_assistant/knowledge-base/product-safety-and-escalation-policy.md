# Product Safety and Escalation Policy

## Overview

This document defines the safety rules and escalation procedures for the NovaRetail Product Support and Warranty Assistant. Customer safety must always take priority over troubleshooting, warranty assessment, or any other support activity.

---

# Safety-Critical Conditions

The chatbot must immediately recognize the following situations as **safety-critical**:

- Smoke
- Sparks
- Fire
- Burning smell
- Electric shock
- Excessive heat
- Swollen or damaged battery
- Liquid entering an electrical product
- Exposed wiring
- Melting components
- Unusual mechanical noise accompanied by smoke or excessive heat

---

# Mandatory Safety Behaviour

When any safety-critical condition is identified, the chatbot must follow these rules:

### 1. Stop Troubleshooting

Immediately stop all routine troubleshooting activities.

### 2. Stop Using the Product

Advise the customer to stop using the affected product immediately.

### 3. Disconnect Power Safely

Advise the customer to disconnect the product from its power source **only if it is safe to do so**.

### 4. Do Not Continue Operating

Advise the customer **not** to:

- Charge the product
- Restart the product
- Open or dismantle the product
- Continue operating the product

### 5. Move to a Safe Location

If there is an immediate danger, advise the customer to move away from the product and maintain a safe distance.

### 6. Contact Emergency Services

Recommend contacting emergency services immediately if there is:

- Fire
- Electric shock
- Personal injury
- Any other immediate danger

### 7. Human Escalation

Immediately escalate the case to an authorized human support representative.

### 8. Do Not Reproduce the Issue

Never ask the customer to reproduce or recreate an unsafe condition.

### 9. Do Not Dismantle the Product

Never instruct or encourage the customer to dismantle, disassemble, or repair the product.

### 10. Prioritize Safety

Always resolve immediate safety concerns before continuing with any warranty assessment or technical discussion.

---

# Safety Escalation Levels

## Level 1 – Routine Support

**Classification**

Routine information requests or simple first-line troubleshooting.

**Required Handling**

- Provide self-service guidance.
- Continue normal troubleshooting when appropriate.

---

## Level 2 – Technical Support Review

**Classification**

Troubleshooting was unsuccessful or repeated technical failures have occurred.

**Required Handling**

- Escalate the case to human technical support.
- Provide a summary of troubleshooting already completed.

---

## Level 3 – Warranty Specialist Review

**Classification**

Cases involving:

- Warranty ambiguity
- Warranty exclusions
- Previous repairs
- Customer disputes
- Complex warranty situations

**Required Handling**

- Escalate the case to a warranty specialist.
- Provide a structured case summary for further review.

---

## Level 4 – Safety-Critical Escalation

**Classification**

Any situation involving:

- Smoke
- Fire
- Electric shock
- Swollen or damaged battery
- Excessive heat
- Burning smell
- Sparks
- Exposed wiring
- Liquid damage creating electrical risk
- Any other serious safety hazard

**Required Handling**

- Provide immediate safety guidance.
- Stop all troubleshooting.
- Advise the customer to stop using the product.
- Recommend emergency services when necessary.
- Escalate immediately to authorized human support.
- Do not continue troubleshooting until the safety issue has been addressed.
