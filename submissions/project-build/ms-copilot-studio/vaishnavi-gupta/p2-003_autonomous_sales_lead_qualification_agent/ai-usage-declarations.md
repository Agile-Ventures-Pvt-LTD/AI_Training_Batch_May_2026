# AI Usage Declaration

## Project Title

**NovaWorks Autonomous Sales Lead Qualification Agent**

### Declaration

This project uses **Microsoft Copilot Studio** to automate the sales lead qualification process. AI is used to interpret customer emails, extract relevant information, apply company-defined qualification rules, generate standardized reports, and assist in customer communication.

All business decisions are based solely on the provided knowledge documents and operational Excel data. The AI does not fabricate information, modify business rules, or make commitments beyond documented company policies.

Microsoft 365 connectors, including **Excel Online (Business)**, **Microsoft Word Online (Business)**, and **Office 365 Outlook**, are used to retrieve operational data, generate reports, update records, and send email communications.

The solution is designed to support business process automation while maintaining accuracy, consistency, security, and compliance with organizational policies.

## AI tool used 
- ChatGPT(OpenAI)
