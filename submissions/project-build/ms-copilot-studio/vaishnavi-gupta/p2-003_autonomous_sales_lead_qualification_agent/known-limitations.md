# Known Limitations

## Project Title

**NovaWorks Autonomous Sales Lead Qualification Agent**

### Limitations

- Depends on Microsoft 365 services and connector availability.
- Requires the operational Excel workbook and tables to be correctly configured.
- Cannot process leads with insufficient mandatory information.
- Cannot make decisions outside the documented qualification rules.
- Does not use external web sources or third-party data.
- Requires manual review when operational data is missing or conflicting.
- Cannot guarantee successful processing if connector authentication or permissions fail.
- Does not make commercial commitments such as pricing, discounts, or delivery dates.
