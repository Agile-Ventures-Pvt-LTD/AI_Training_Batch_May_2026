# Custom Topic Design

## Topic 1: Guided Product Troubleshooting and Safety Triage

Purpose:
Provide safe troubleshooting support for supported laptops and printers with safety checks and escalation.

## Flow

1. Collect Product Details
- Product Family
- Product Model
- Issue Category
- Symptoms

2. Safety Assessment
- Check smoke, fire, electric shock, battery damage, liquid exposure, and other risks.
- Safety risk → Stop troubleshooting and Level 4 escalation.

3. Product Validation
- Verify supported model.
- Unknown/unsupported model → Provide limitation and escalation.

4. Troubleshooting Branch

Laptop:
- Lenovo source
- Power, charging, battery, display, Wi-Fi, overheating issues

Printer:
- HP source
- Printing, scanning, connectivity, paper jam issues

5. Controlled Troubleshooting
- Track steps.
- Avoid repetition.
- Stop when resolved or maximum attempts reached.

6. Case Summary
Capture:
- Product details
- Issue
- Safety status
- Troubleshooting performed
- Outcome
- Escalation level

## Topic 2: Warranty Assessment

Purpose:
Provide preliminary warranty evaluation using NovaCare policy.

Flow:
- Validate purchase details.
- Check coverage period.
- Identify exclusions.
- Assign service route.
- Escalate when required.

## Outcomes

- Self-service resolved
- Technical support review
- Warranty specialist review
- Unsupported product escalation
- Safety-critical escalation
