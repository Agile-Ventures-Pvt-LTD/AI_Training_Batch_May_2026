# Known Limitations

## Current Limitations

- The agent supports only configured products and models.
- Model-specific guidance is limited to available manufacturer sources.
- The agent cannot access live repair status, inventory, order history, payment records, or warranty databases.
- The agent cannot approve or reject final warranty claims.
- The agent cannot perform remote access or repair actions.
- The agent cannot provide unsupported product specifications.
- Safety-critical cases require human support escalation.
- Missing customer information may require additional details before assessment.
- Warranty assessment is preliminary and requires authorised human review for final decisions.
