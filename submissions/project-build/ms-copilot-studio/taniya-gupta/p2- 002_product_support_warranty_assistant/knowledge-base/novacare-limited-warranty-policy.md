# NovaCare Limited Warranty Policy

**Policy Version:** 1.0
**Effective Date:** 1 July 2026
**Company:** NovaRetail Technologies Pvt. Ltd.

---

## Policy Clauses

1. NovaCare warranty guidance applies only to eligible products purchased directly from NovaRetail Technologies.
2. The standard product warranty is **12 months** from the original invoice date.
3. A valid purchase invoice is normally required for warranty validation.
4. The product serial number must correspond to the product and invoice.
5. Products reported within **seven calendar days** of delivery may qualify for **dead-on-arrival (DOA) assessment** when a hardware defect prevents normal intended use.
6. Dead-on-arrival assessment does not automatically guarantee replacement.
7. Replacement requires technical verification and approval by an authorised representative.
8. Standard warranty coverage applies to manufacturing defects occurring during normal intended use.
9. Standard warranty does **not** normally cover: accidental damage, liquid damage, fire damage, external electrical surges, misuse, negligence, improper storage, unauthorised repair, unauthorised modification, incompatible accessories, non-functional cosmetic damage, normal wear and tear, software configuration problems, viruses or malware, data loss, or consumable items.
10. Laptop batteries and bundled accessories are covered for **6 months** from the original invoice date.
11. Printer toner, paper, and other consumables are **not covered** by the standard product warranty.
12. Damage potentially caused by unsupported consumables requires human review.
13. Warranty service does **not** include data backup, recovery, restoration, or migration.
14. Customers are responsible for backing up data before submitting a device for service.
15. Basic approved troubleshooting must normally be completed before repair escalation.
16. Customers must not be asked to share passwords, encryption keys, banking information, or unrelated personal files.
17. Warranty guidance generated by the chatbot is **preliminary**.
18. Final warranty approval, rejection, repair, replacement, or commercial settlement must be completed by an **authorised human representative**.
19. The chatbot must not state that a repair, replacement, or warranty request has been submitted unless an integrated system successfully performs the action.
20. Cases involving unclear damage causes, conflicting evidence, multiple previous repairs, or customer disputes must be escalated for human review.

## Warranty Exclusions (Clause 9 — Detailed)

The following conditions are **not covered** under the standard NovaCare warranty:

- Accidental physical damage (drops, impacts, cracks)
- Liquid damage (spills, submersion, moisture ingress)
- Fire or heat damage
- External electrical surges or power-related damage
- Misuse or use outside intended purpose
- Negligence or improper storage
- Unauthorised repair by a non-approved service provider
- Unauthorised modification or alteration
- Use of incompatible accessories or components
- Non-functional cosmetic damage (scratches, dents that do not affect operation)
- Normal wear and tear
- Software configuration problems, OS reinstallation issues
- Viruses, malware, or ransomware damage
- Data loss (regardless of cause)
- Consumable items (toner, ink, paper, batteries not bundled at time of purchase)

## Dead-on-Arrival (DOA) Assessment Requirements

For a case to be assessed as potential DOA, **all** of the following must apply:

1. Product was purchased directly from NovaRetail Technologies
2. Issue was reported within **7 calendar days** of the delivery date
3. A hardware defect prevents normal intended use
4. No accidental or liquid damage is present
5. Proof of purchase (invoice) is available
6. Serial number corresponds to the product and invoice

> **Important:** Qualifying for DOA assessment does not guarantee replacement. An authorised representative must verify and approve.

## Dispute and Escalation

- Cases with unclear or conflicting damage evidence → human review required
- Cases with multiple prior repairs for the same issue → warranty specialist escalation
- Customer disputes with the preliminary assessment → allow correction, then escalate unresolved disputes
- Safety-critical cases → immediate escalation, bypass normal warranty questioning

---

## Chatbot Limitations

The NovaCare Assist chatbot:
- Provides **preliminary assessments only**
- Does **not** have access to live warranty databases
- Does **not** have access to repair status, order history, or inventory
- Does **not** approve or reject warranty claims
- Does **not** submit repair or replacement requests
- Does **not** provide legal conclusions

All final decisions are made by authorised NovaRetail representatives.
