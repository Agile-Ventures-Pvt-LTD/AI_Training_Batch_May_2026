# Product Safety and Escalation Policy

**Version:** 1.0
**Effective Date:** 1 July 2026
**Company:** NovaRetail Technologies Pvt. Ltd.

---

## Safety-Critical Conditions

The following conditions require an **immediate safety response**. When any of these are reported, stop all routine troubleshooting immediately:

| Condition | Description |
|---|---|
| Smoke | Visible smoke from the product or components |
| Sparks | Visible sparks or electrical arcing |
| Fire | Any visible flame or fire from the product |
| Burning smell | Burning, melting, or acrid smell from the product |
| Electric shock | Customer reported electric shock or tingling sensation when touching the product |
| Excessive heat | Product surface is too hot to touch; heat beyond normal operating temperature |
| Swollen or damaged battery | Battery is visibly bulging, swollen, deformed, cracked, or leaking |
| Liquid entering an electrical product | Water, beverage, or any liquid has entered the product body |
| Exposed wiring | Visible damaged, frayed, or exposed electrical wiring |
| Melting components | Any component showing signs of melting or heat distortion |
| Unusual mechanical noise accompanied by smoke or heat | Grinding, clicking, or popping sounds combined with heat or smoke |

---

## Mandatory Safety Behaviour

When a safety-critical condition is identified or reported by the customer, the chatbot **must**:

1. **Stop routine troubleshooting immediately.** Do not proceed with normal diagnostic steps.
2. **Advise the customer to stop using the product** without delay.
3. **Advise disconnection from power** — only if it is safe to do so. Do not instruct disconnection if the customer is at risk of electric shock.
4. **Advise against** charging, restarting, opening, or continuing to operate the product.
5. **Advise the customer to move away** from the product when immediate danger exists.
6. **Recommend emergency services** (fire brigade, medical assistance) when there is fire, injury, electric shock, or immediate danger to people.
7. **Direct the case to urgent human support** — Level 4 escalation.
8. **Do not ask the customer to reproduce the unsafe condition** under any circumstances.
9. **Do not ask the customer to dismantle the product** in any way.
10. **Address immediate safety before continuing** with any warranty questioning or troubleshooting.

## Escalation Levels

| Level | Classification | Typical Scenarios | Required Handling |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Level 1** | Routine information or simple first-line troubleshooting | Product setup, basic configuration, general policy questions | Self-service guidance through chatbot |
| **Level 2** | Troubleshooting unsuccessful or repeated technical failure | Issue not resolved after all troubleshooting steps, same fault returning | Human technical-support review — direct to NovaRetail support |
| **Level 3** | Warranty ambiguity, exclusion dispute, previous repair, or customer disagreement | Unclear damage cause, customer disputes assessment, multiple prior repairs | Warranty specialist review — escalate to NovaRetail warranty team |
| **Level 4** | Smoke, fire, electric shock, swollen battery, or other immediate safety risk | Any condition listed in the Safety-Critical Conditions table above | **Immediate safety guidance and urgent escalation — do not delay** |

---

## Safety Response Template

When a Level 4 safety-critical condition is identified, use a response that includes **all** of the following elements:

- Acknowledge the seriousness of the situation
- Instruct the customer to stop using the product
- Advise safe power disconnection (where applicable)
- Advise against any further product interaction
- Recommend moving away if there is immediate physical danger
- Recommend contacting emergency services if fire, injury, or electric shock is involved
- Confirm that this case is being escalated to urgent human support
- Do NOT continue with troubleshooting or warranty questions

---

## Safety Escalation Trigger Conditions

The chatbot must check for safety-critical conditions:

- **At the start of every troubleshooting session** — before any technical guidance is provided
- **Whenever the customer mentions** physical damage, heat, smell, or any unusual product behaviour
- **During warranty assessment** — if safety-relevant information is disclosed

---

## Privacy During Safety Interactions

During a safety-critical interaction, the chatbot must **not** ask for:

- Passwords or PINs
- Banking or payment information
- Full serial numbers (in documentation or screenshots)
- Medical information
- Unrelated personal data

---

## Escalation Contact

For all escalations, direct the customer to:

- **NovaRetail Customer Support:** Available through official NovaRetail contact channels
- **Level 4 Safety:** Emergency services (fire brigade, ambulance, police) should be contacted first where there is immediate danger to life or property
- **Level 3 Warranty Specialist:** NovaRetail authorised warranty service centre

---

## Important Limitations

- The chatbot does **not** have access to emergency dispatch services
- The chatbot does **not** have access to real-time case tracking or repair status
- The chatbot does **not** make final warranty decisions
- All Level 3 and Level 4 escalations require human review and resolution
