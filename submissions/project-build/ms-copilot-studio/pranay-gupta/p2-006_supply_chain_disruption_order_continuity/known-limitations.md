# Known Limitations

## Evidence
Actual test results, screenshots, approvals, notifications and tool outcomes must come from real execution.

## Synthetic Data
The project uses supplied synthetic operational data.

## Human Approval
Approval-dependent actions remain blocked until actual approval is available.

## Unapproved Suppliers
Unapproved suppliers cannot be autonomously selected.

## Customer Actions
The system does not autonomously cancel orders, change customer commitments or promise delivery dates.

## Commercial Actions
The system does not autonomously authorize commercial expenditure.

## Tool Failures
Failed Word, Excel or Outlook operations must be recorded as failures and must not be represented as successful.

## Reassessment
Automated reassessment is bounded; unresolved cases move to Manual Review.
