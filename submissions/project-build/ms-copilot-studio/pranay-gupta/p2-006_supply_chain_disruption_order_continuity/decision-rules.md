# Decision Rules

## ATP
`ATP = On Hand - Reserved + Inbound Within 7 Days - Quality Hold`

Quality-held quantity is unusable supply.

## Safety Stock
Safety stock may be consumed only when a Strategic or SLA-protected order is at risk.

## Customer Priority
1. Strategic + SLA protected
2. Priority
3. Standard

All open affected orders should be evaluated.

## Alternate Supplier
Unapproved suppliers cannot be autonomously selected.

## Commercial Approval
- Alternate premium >15% → Finance Business Partner.
- Expedite premium >10% → Supply Chain Director.

## Conflict Precedence
Safety/quality → Strategic/SLA → supplier approval → inventory → commercial approval → cost → lower-priority convenience.

## No Viable Route
Use Management Escalation and Critical risk where appropriate.

## High/Critical
Every High/Critical classification must explain why.

## Autonomous Safety
No autonomous PO placement, supplier approval, order cancellation, customer-date commitment or commercial-spend authorization.
