# Known Limitations

## Overview

The NovaRetail Product Support & Warranty Assistant provides first-line product support and preliminary warranty guidance within the configured project scope. The following limitations apply.

---

## Current Limitations

| Area | Limitation |
|------|------------|
| Product Support | Supports only the configured Lenovo ThinkPad E14 Gen 5, ThinkPad E16 Gen 1, and HP LaserJet Pro M428–M429 series. |
| Warranty | Provides preliminary assessments only and cannot approve or reject warranty claims. |
| Customer Data | Cannot access customer accounts, purchase history, or personal information. |
| Live Systems | No integration with warranty databases, repair systems, or inventory management. |
| Repair Services | Cannot create repair requests, schedule service appointments, or track repair status. |
| Order Management | Cannot access orders, invoices, shipping, or delivery information. |
| Knowledge Scope | Responses are limited to the configured knowledge sources and policies. |
| Human Decisions | Final warranty decisions, repairs, and replacements require authorised NovaRetail representatives. |

---

## Out of Scope

- Warranty approval or rejection
- Repair or replacement authorisation
- Live warranty lookup
- Order and shipment tracking
- Customer account management
- Payment or billing support
- Products outside the supported scope

---
