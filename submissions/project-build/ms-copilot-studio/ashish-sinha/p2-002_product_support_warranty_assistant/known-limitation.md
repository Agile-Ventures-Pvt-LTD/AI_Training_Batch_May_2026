# Product Support & Warranty Assistant

## Version

1.0

## Platform

Microsoft Copilot Studio

## Current Scope

Lenovo ThinkPad E14 Gen 5 (Laptop Support Only)

---

# Purpose

This document outlines the known limitations of the Product Support & Warranty Assistant. These limitations define the system boundaries and clarify capabilities that are intentionally excluded from the current implementation.

---

# 1. No Live Warranty Lookup

The assistant does not connect to a live warranty management system.

### Limitation

- Cannot verify active warranty status.
- Cannot retrieve warranty expiry dates.
- Cannot confirm warranty registration.
- Cannot access customer purchase history.

### Impact

All warranty assessments are **preliminary** and require verification by an authorized warranty specialist.

---

# 2. No Case Creation

The assistant cannot create or submit service requests.

### Limitation

- Cannot create support tickets.
- Cannot generate repair requests.
- Cannot submit warranty claims.
- Cannot assign cases to support teams.

### Impact

Customers must contact the official support team to create service requests after completing the conversation.

---

# 3. No Repair Status Tracking

The assistant has no visibility into existing repair records.

### Limitation

- Cannot check repair progress.
- Cannot provide repair completion dates.
- Cannot identify technician assignments.
- Cannot track shipment or return status.

### Impact

Customers requiring repair updates must contact customer support directly.

---

# 4. Limited Product Coverage

The assistant currently supports only one laptop model.

### Supported Product

- Lenovo ThinkPad E14 Gen 5

### Not Supported

- Other Lenovo laptop models
- HP printers
- Desktop computers
- Monitors
- Accessories
- Third-party products

### Impact

Unsupported products receive general guidance and are redirected to the appropriate support channel.

---

# 5. No Final Warranty Decisions

The assistant performs only a preliminary warranty assessment.

### Limitation

The assistant cannot:

- Approve warranty claims.
- Reject warranty claims.
- Authorize repairs.
- Authorize replacements.
- Make legal warranty decisions.

### Impact

Final warranty decisions are made by authorized warranty specialists after reviewing all required documentation.

---

# 6. No Emergency Service Integration

The assistant is not integrated with emergency or safety response services.

### Limitation

The assistant cannot:

- Contact emergency responders.
- Dispatch field technicians.
- Notify emergency services.
- Initiate urgent hardware recalls.

### Impact

For safety-critical situations, customers are instructed to stop using the product and contact the appropriate emergency or authorized support services.

---

# 7. No Inventory Visibility

The assistant has no access to inventory management systems.

### Limitation

The assistant cannot:

- Check spare part availability.
- Verify replacement stock.
- Confirm product availability.
- Estimate shipping times.
- Reserve replacement units.

### Impact

Inventory-related enquiries must be handled by customer service or sales representatives.

---

# 8. No Remote Diagnostics

The assistant cannot remotely inspect or control customer devices.

### Limitation

- No remote desktop access.
- No remote hardware diagnostics.
- No log collection.
- No automated system scans.

### Impact

Troubleshooting is limited to customer-provided information and approved guidance.

---

# 9. No Customer Account Access

The assistant does not access personal customer accounts.

### Limitation

The assistant cannot retrieve:

- Order history
- Customer profiles
- Registered devices
- Service history
- Account preferences

### Impact

Responses are based only on information provided during the conversation.

---

# 10. Knowledge Base Limitations

The assistant uses only approved knowledge sources.

### Limitation

It does not use:

- Public internet searches
- Community forums
- Third-party repair guides
- Unverified technical content

### Impact

If the required information is unavailable in the approved knowledge sources, the assistant recommends contacting authorized support.

---

# 11. Safety Limitations

The assistant prioritizes customer safety but cannot physically verify reported conditions.

### Limitation

Safety assessments are based entirely on customer responses.

### Impact

The assistant may recommend escalation whenever safety information is incomplete or uncertain.

---

# 12. Future Enhancements

The following capabilities may be added in future releases:

- Live warranty verification
- CRM integration
- Automatic support ticket creation
- Repair status tracking
- Inventory management integration
- Additional Lenovo laptop models
- HP printer support
- Microsoft Teams integration
- Power Automate workflows
- Voice-enabled interactions
- Customer sentiment analysis
- Analytics dashboard

---

# Summary

The Product Support & Warranty Assistant is designed to provide safe, structured, and policy-compliant first-line support. It performs guided troubleshooting and preliminary warranty assessment within defined operational boundaries.

Any activity requiring live enterprise data, warranty authorization, repair management, inventory access, or emergency response is intentionally excluded from the current implementation and must be handled by authorized personnel or enterprise systems.
