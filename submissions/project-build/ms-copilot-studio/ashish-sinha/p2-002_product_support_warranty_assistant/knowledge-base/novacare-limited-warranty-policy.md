# NovaCare Limited Warranty Policy

**Document Version:** 1.0  
**Effective Date:** 1 July 2026  
**Company:** NovaRetail Technologies Pvt. Ltd.  
**Document Type:** Internal Customer Support Knowledge Base  
**Knowledge Source:** Warranty & Service Policy

---

# Purpose

This document defines the official NovaCare Limited Warranty Policy used by the Product Support & Warranty Assistant.

The chatbot uses this document to provide **preliminary warranty guidance only**. It must **never approve or reject warranty claims**, promise repairs or replacements, or claim that a warranty request has been submitted.

Final warranty decisions are made only by an authorized NovaRetail representative.

---

# Scope

This policy applies only to eligible products purchased directly from **NovaRetail Technologies Pvt. Ltd.**

Supported product categories include:

- Lenovo ThinkPad E14 Gen 5 Laptop
- HP LaserJet Pro MFP M428–M429 Printer
- Bundled Laptop Battery
- Bundled Charger
- Bundled Printer Power Cable

---

# Standard Warranty Coverage

## Laptop & Printer

Coverage Period

- 12 months from the original invoice date.

Coverage Includes

- Manufacturing defects
- Hardware failures during normal intended use
- Factory component defects

---

## Laptop Battery

Coverage Period

- 6 months from the original invoice date.

---

## Bundled Accessories

Coverage Period

- 6 months from the original invoice date.

Examples

- Laptop charger
- Printer power cable

---

## Consumables

The following items are **not covered** under the standard product warranty:

- Printer toner
- Printer paper
- Other consumable supplies

---

# Required Documents

Customers are normally expected to provide:

- Valid purchase invoice
- Product serial number

The serial number should correspond to both:

- the purchased product
- the purchase invoice

---

# Dead-on-Arrival (DOA) Assessment

A product may qualify for **Dead-on-Arrival (DOA) assessment** when all of the following conditions are met:

- Purchased directly from NovaRetail
- Reported within seven calendar days of delivery
- Hardware defect prevents normal intended use

Important:

DOA assessment **does not automatically guarantee** replacement.

Replacement requires:

- Technical verification
- Human approval
- Authorized NovaRetail review

---

# Standard Warranty Covers

The standard warranty generally covers:

- Manufacturing defects
- Hardware failures during normal intended use
- Factory component issues

---

# Standard Warranty Exclusions

The standard warranty normally does **not** cover:

- Accidental damage
- Liquid damage
- Fire damage
- External electrical surge
- Misuse
- Negligence
- Improper storage
- Unauthorized repair
- Unauthorized modification
- Incompatible accessories
- Cosmetic damage
- Normal wear and tear
- Software configuration problems
- Virus infection
- Malware
- Data loss
- Consumable items

Cases involving unsupported consumables require human review.

---

# Data Protection

Warranty service does **not** include:

- Data backup
- Data recovery
- Data restoration
- Data migration

Customers remain responsible for backing up their own data before submitting any product for service.

---

# Troubleshooting Requirement

Before repair escalation:

- Basic approved troubleshooting should normally be completed.
- Safety concerns always take priority over troubleshooting.

---

# Customer Privacy

The chatbot must never request:

- Passwords
- Banking information
- Encryption keys
- Unrelated personal files

Only information required for preliminary warranty assessment should be collected.

---

# Warranty Decision Boundaries

The chatbot may:

- Explain warranty rules
- Identify possible coverage
- Explain exclusions
- Identify missing documents
- Recommend next steps

The chatbot must **not**:

- Approve warranty claims
- Reject warranty claims
- Promise free repair
- Promise replacement
- Claim that a service request has been created
- Claim live access to warranty systems

---

# Human Review Required

The chatbot must recommend human review when:

- Damage cause is unclear
- Evidence is conflicting
- Multiple previous repairs exist
- Customer disputes the preliminary assessment
- Unsupported consumables may have caused damage

---

# Preliminary Warranty Classification

The assistant may classify a case as one of the following:

- Potentially Covered
- Potential Dead-on-Arrival Assessment
- Potentially Excluded
- Outside Standard Coverage
- Insufficient Information
- Human Review Required
- Safety-Critical Escalation

These classifications are **preliminary** and do not represent a final warranty decision.
