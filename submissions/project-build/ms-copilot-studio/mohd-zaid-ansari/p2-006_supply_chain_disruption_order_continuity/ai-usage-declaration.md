# AI Usage Declaration

## Project

**NovaSphere Supply Chain Continuity Management System using Microsoft Copilot Studio**

---

## AI Usage

AI tools were used as permitted to support:

* Understanding project requirements.
* Designing agent architecture.
* Defining specialist responsibilities.
* Creating topic logic and decision rules.
* Preparing documentation and test scenarios.

---

## Responsibility

The participant remains responsible for:

* Solution implementation.
* Copilot Studio configuration.
* Testing and validation.
* Business rule accuracy.

---

## AI Safety Controls

The solution must not:

* Fabricate supplier approvals.
* Fabricate customer agreements.
* Claim purchase orders were created.
* Claim approvals were received.
* Fabricate test evidence or screenshots.

---

## Data Usage

Only the provided synthetic NovaSphere dataset is used.

---

## Human Oversight

AI provides recommendations only.

Human approval remains required for:

* Supplier qualification.
* Commercial decisions.
* Customer commitments.
* Management escalation.

---

## Compliance

The solution follows:

* Deterministic business rules.
* Explainable decisions.
* Traceable outputs.
* Human approval boundaries.
