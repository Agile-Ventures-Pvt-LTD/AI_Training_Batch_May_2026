# Solution Summary

## Project Title

**AI-Powered Supply Chain Continuity Management System using Microsoft Copilot Studio**

---

# Overview

This solution is an autonomous AI-driven Supply Chain Continuity Management System developed using **Microsoft Copilot Studio** and **Excel Online (Business)**.

The system continuously monitors supply disruption requests, validates disruption information, coordinates multiple AI specialist agents, evaluates operational and commercial impacts, recommends recovery strategies, verifies approval requirements, generates stakeholder reports, and updates disruption statuses.

The solution follows a **Supervisor–Specialist Multi-Agent Architecture**, where the **Supply Continuity Supervisor** orchestrates all topics, tools, and specialist agents.

---

# Business Problem

Supply chain disruptions can delay production, affect customer commitments, increase operational costs, and impact revenue.

Traditional disruption management requires manual coordination between procurement, inventory, logistics, commercial teams, and management, resulting in slower response times and inconsistent decision-making.

This solution automates disruption assessment and recovery planning while supporting informed business decisions.

# Workflow Summary

## Phase 1

Recurrence Trigger starts the workflow.

---

## Phase 2

Supervisor invokes

```
/Disruption Intake & Validation
```

to retrieve and validate disruption information.

---

## Phase 3

Supervisor invokes

- /Inventory Impact Specialist
- /Alternate Supplier Specialist
- /Customer & Order Impact Specialist
- /Commercial Impact Specialist

to perform parallel impact assessments.

---

## Phase 4

Supervisor invokes

```
/Recovery Planning Specialist
```

to generate ranked recovery strategies.

---

## Phase 5

Supervisor invokes

```
/Recovery Strategy Resolution
```

to select the most suitable recovery strategy.

---

## Phase 6

Supervisor invokes

```
/Approval Exception Reassessment
```

to validate approval and escalation requirements.

---

## Phase 7

Supervisor invokes

```
/Reporting & Communication Specialist
```

to prepare executive summaries and stakeholder communications.

---

## Phase 8

Supervisor invokes

```
/Update Disruption Status
```

to update the disruption request status.
