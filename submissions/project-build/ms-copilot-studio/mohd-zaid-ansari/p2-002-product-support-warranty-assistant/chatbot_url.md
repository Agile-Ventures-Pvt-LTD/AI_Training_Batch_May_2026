# Opening Instructions

1. Open the published chatbot URL in any modern web browser.
2. Wait for the chatbot to load.
3. Start a conversation by typing a greeting such as **"Hi"** or **"Hello"**.
4. Ask product support or warranty-related questions.
5. Follow the chatbot prompts to complete troubleshooting or warranty assessment.
6. Review the final recommendation or case summary provided by the chatbot.

# Supported Scenarios

- Laptop troubleshooting
- Printer troubleshooting
- Product safety assessment
- Preliminary warranty assessment
- Dead-on-Arrival (DOA) guidance
- Warranty service route recommendation

# Limitations

- Supports only NovaRetail laptops and printers.
- Provides **preliminary** warranty guidance only.
- Cannot approve or reject warranty claims.
- Cannot create, modify, or track support tickets.
- Cannot access live warranty, repair, inventory, payment, or order databases.
- Does not schedule repairs or technician visits.
- Cannot process refunds or replacements.
- Safety-critical issues are always escalated to human support.
- Responses are limited to the configured knowledge sources and project scope.
