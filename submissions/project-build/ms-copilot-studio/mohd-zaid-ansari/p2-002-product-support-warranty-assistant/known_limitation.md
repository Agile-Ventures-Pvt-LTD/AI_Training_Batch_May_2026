# Known Limitations

The NovaRetail AI Support Agent has the following limitations:

- No live warranty, repair, order, payment, or inventory lookup.
- Cannot create, update, or track support cases.
- Cannot check repair or service request status.
- Supports only laptops and printers within the defined project scope.
- Cannot approve or reject warranty claims.
- Cannot guarantee repair, replacement, or refund eligibility.
- No integration with emergency services or external support systems.
- No visibility into product inventory or stock availability.
- Responses are limited to the configured knowledge sources.
- Human review is required for final warranty and service decisions.
