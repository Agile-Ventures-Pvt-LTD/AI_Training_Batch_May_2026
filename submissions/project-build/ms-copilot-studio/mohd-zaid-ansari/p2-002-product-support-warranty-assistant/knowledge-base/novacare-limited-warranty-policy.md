# NovaCare Limited Warranty Policy

**Policy Version:** 1.0  
**Effective Date:** 1 July 2026  
**Company:** NovaRetail Technologies Pvt. Ltd.

## Warranty Policy

1. NovaCare warranty guidance applies only to eligible products purchased directly from NovaRetail Technologies.

2. The standard product warranty is **12 months** from the original invoice date.

3. A valid purchase invoice is normally required for warranty validation.

4. The product serial number must correspond to the product and invoice.

5. Products reported within **seven (7) calendar days** of delivery may qualify for **Dead-on-Arrival (DOA)** assessment when a hardware defect prevents normal intended use.

6. Dead-on-Arrival assessment does **not** automatically guarantee replacement.

7. Replacement requires technical verification and approval by an authorised representative.

8. Standard warranty coverage applies to manufacturing defects occurring during normal intended use.

9. Standard warranty does **not** normally cover:
   - Accidental damage
   - Liquid damage
   - Fire damage
   - External electrical surges
   - Misuse
   - Negligence
   - Improper storage
   - Unauthorised repair
   - Unauthorised modification
   - Incompatible accessories
   - Non-functional cosmetic damage
   - Normal wear and tear
   - Software configuration problems
   - Viruses or malware
   - Data loss
   - Consumable items

10. Laptop batteries and bundled accessories are covered for **six (6) months** from the original invoice date.

11. Printer toner, paper, and other consumables are **not** covered by the standard product warranty.

12. Damage potentially caused by unsupported consumables requires human review.

13. Warranty service does **not** include:
    - Data backup
    - Data recovery
    - Data restoration
    - Data migration

14. Customers are responsible for backing up their data before submitting a device for service.

15. Basic approved troubleshooting must normally be completed before repair escalation.

16. Customers must **not** be asked to share:
    - Passwords
    - Encryption keys
    - Banking information
    - Unrelated personal files

17. Warranty guidance generated by the chatbot is preliminary.

18. Final warranty approval, rejection, repair, replacement, or commercial settlement must be completed by an authorised human representative.

19. The chatbot must **not** state that a repair, replacement, or warranty request has been submitted unless an integrated system has successfully performed the action.

20. Cases involving any of the following must be escalated for human review:
    - Unclear damage causes
    - Conflicting evidence
    - Multiple previous repairs
    - Customer disputes
