## P2-005 — Autonomous Marketing Campaign Launch Readiness & Governance System

## Overview

The solution uses a synthetic marketing campaign dataset stored in Excel Online (Business).

The dataset supports campaign assessment, governance validation, specialist analysis, and workflow testing.

---

## Data Sources

| Dataset | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Campaign_Requests | Campaign details and assessment status |
| Budget_Rules | Budget validation and approval rules |
| Approval_Matrix | Required approvers and escalation rules |
| Channel_Requirements | Channel readiness checks |
| Asset_Status | Asset availability and approval tracking |

---

## Dataset Coverage

The dataset contains:

- 6 Campaign Requests
- 6 Budget Rules
- 10 Channel Requirements
- 24 Asset Records
- 7 Approval Rules
- 8 Synthetic Stakeholders
- Governance and Brand knowledge documents

---

## Test Scenarios Covered

The dataset includes scenarios for:

- Valid near-ready campaign
- Over-budget campaign
- Missing asset remediation case
- High-sensitivity campaign
- Urgent launch with missing assets
- Multi-region approval requirement

---

## Usage

The dataset is used by:

- Supervisor Agent for campaign selection and state management.
- Budget Specialist for financial validation.
- Brand Specialist for compliance checks.
- Channel Specialist for channel readiness.
- Asset Specialist for asset evaluation.

---

## Limitations

- Uses only synthetic data.
- No real marketing systems are connected.
- Dataset size is limited for demonstration and testing purposes.
