# Known Limitations

## Overview

This chatbot is designed to provide guided troubleshooting and preliminary warranty assessments. It has the following limitations:

- Supports only Lenovo laptops and HP printers.
- Does not access live warranty or repair databases.
- Does not create or track service requests.
- Does not approve or reject warranty claims.
- Cannot guarantee repair, replacement, or reimbursement.
- Warranty assessments are preliminary and require human review.
- Responses are limited to the configured knowledge sources.
- Cannot answer questions outside the product support and warranty domain.
- Human intervention is required for safety-critical cases and complex warranty disputes.
