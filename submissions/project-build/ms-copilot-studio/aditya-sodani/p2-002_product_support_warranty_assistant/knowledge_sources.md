# Knowledge Sources

## Overview

The chatbot uses official product documentation as its primary source of information to provide accurate troubleshooting guidance.

## Knowledge Sources

### Lenovo Product Manuals

Used for:
- Laptop features
- Hardware troubleshooting
- Power and battery issues
- Display, keyboard, and connectivity guidance
- Safety recommendations

### HP Product Manuals

Used for:
- Printer features
- Paper jam troubleshooting
- Print quality issues
- Network and connectivity setup
- Scanner and cartridge guidance

## Source Priority

When responding to customer queries, the chatbot prioritizes official Lenovo and HP product manuals over general knowledge to ensure accurate and reliable guidance.

## Limitations

The chatbot does not access live manufacturer databases or online support systems. Responses are based only on the knowledge sources configured in Microsoft Copilot Studio.
