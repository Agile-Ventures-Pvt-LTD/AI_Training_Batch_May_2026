# NovaCare Limited Warranty Policy

**Policy Version:** 1.0  
**Effective Date:** 1 July 2026  
**Company:** NovaRetail Technologies Pvt. Ltd.

## Standard Warranty

- Standard warranty applies only to eligible products purchased directly from NovaRetail Technologies.
- Standard warranty period is **12 months** from the original purchase invoice date.
- A valid purchase invoice is normally required.
- Product serial number should match the invoice.

## Dead on Arrival (DOA)

A product may qualify for DOA assessment if:

- Reported within 7 calendar days of delivery
- Hardware defect prevents normal operation
- Purchased from NovaRetail
- No accidental or liquid damage

DOA assessment does **not** guarantee replacement.

## Warranty Covers

- Manufacturing defects
- Hardware failures during normal usage

## Warranty Does Not Cover

- Accidental damage
- Liquid damage
- Fire damage
- Electrical surge
- Unauthorized repair
- Unauthorized modification
- Cosmetic damage
- Misuse
- Malware
- Software configuration
- Data loss
- Normal wear and tear
- Consumables

## Batteries

Bundled laptop batteries are covered for **6 months**.

## Accessories

Bundled accessories are covered for **6 months**.

## Printer Consumables

Printer toner, paper and consumables are not covered.

## Customer Responsibilities

Customers should:

- Keep invoice safely
- Backup important data
- Complete basic troubleshooting
- Never share passwords or banking information

## Chatbot Limitations

This chatbot only provides a **preliminary warranty assessment**.

Final decisions regarding:

- Warranty approval
- Repair
- Replacement
- Commercial settlement

are made only by authorized NovaRetail representatives.
