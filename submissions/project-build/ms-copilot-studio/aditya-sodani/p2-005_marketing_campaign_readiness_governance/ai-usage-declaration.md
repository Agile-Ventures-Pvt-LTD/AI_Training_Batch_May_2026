# AI Usage Declaration

## Project
P2-005 — Marketing Campaign Readiness & Governance

## Purpose

This document declares the use of Artificial Intelligence tools during the design, development, testing, troubleshooting, and documentation of the P2-005 Marketing Campaign Readiness & Governance project.

## AI Tools Used

The following AI tools were used during project development:

- ChatGPT
- Microsoft Copilot Studio generative AI capabilities

## Usage of ChatGPT

ChatGPT was used as a development assistance tool for:

- Understanding project requirements.
- Breaking the PRD into implementation steps.
- Designing the Supervisor–Specialist architecture.
- Planning agent orchestration.
- Designing custom topic logic.
- Creating and reviewing Power Fx expressions.
- Troubleshooting Copilot Studio errors.
- Designing specialist-agent responsibilities.
- Preparing test scenarios.
- Analyzing evaluation results.
- Preparing project documentation.
- Improving technical explanations and documentation structure.

## Usage of Microsoft Copilot Studio AI

Microsoft Copilot Studio provides the generative AI capabilities used by the implemented agents.

Generative AI is used for:

- Understanding campaign assessment requests.
- Selecting appropriate tools and specialist agents.
- Performing domain-specific specialist reasoning.
- Interpreting governance knowledge.
- Consolidating campaign findings.
- Producing readiness explanations.
- Supporting remediation decisions.
- Generating final reporting and stakeholder communication.

## Human Involvement

AI-generated recommendations, implementation steps, formulas, agent instructions, and documentation were reviewed during project development.

The final implementation decisions, Copilot Studio configuration, agent setup, custom topics, connectors, testing, debugging, and project submission remain under human control.

## Governance Controls

The solution does not rely solely on unrestricted generative AI reasoning.

Important governance controls include:

- Deterministic Campaign Intake & Validation.
- Explicit campaign-status checks.
- Duplicate assessment prevention.
- Mandatory specialist assessments.
- Specialist retry handling.
- Insufficient Evidence handling.
- Mandatory outcome precedence.
- Controlled remediation.
- Maximum two automated reassessment cycles.
- Human approval requirements.
- Supervisor-only final decision authority.
- Final status persistence only after validation.

## Human Approval

The AI system is not permitted to fabricate or assume human approval.

Where governance requires human approval, an actual approval result must be obtained through the configured Approval & Finalisation process.

## AI Decision Boundary

Specialist agents may analyze campaign information and provide recommendations, but they do not have authority to assign the final campaign readiness outcome.

The Campaign Readiness Supervisor is responsible for validating specialist findings and assigning the final readiness outcome according to the defined governance policy.

## AI Limitations

Generative AI outputs may vary between executions.

For this reason:

- Important deterministic checks are implemented using custom topics and structured conditions.
- Mandatory evidence must not be inferred.
- Failed specialist assessments are explicitly handled.
- Human approvals cannot be generated by the AI.
- Governance rules take precedence over generated recommendations.

## Campaign Launch Boundary

The AI solution is designed only to assess campaign readiness.

It does not:

- Launch marketing campaigns.
- Activate advertising campaigns.
- Publish campaign content.
- Deploy campaign assets.
- Activate marketing channels.

A `Ready` outcome represents a readiness assessment and does not indicate that a campaign has been launched.

## Declaration

AI tools were used as supporting technologies during the development of this project. AI-assisted content and recommendations were reviewed and adapted during implementation.

The submitted solution, configuration, testing, documentation, and final project decisions remain the responsibility of the project participant.
