
# Known Limitations

## NovaCare Product Support and Warranty Assistant

This document describes the current limitations of the chatbot implementation.

---

# 1. No Live Warranty Lookup

The assistant does not connect to any live warranty database.

Limitations:

- Cannot verify real-time warranty status.
- Cannot access customer purchase records.
- Cannot retrieve invoice details automatically.
- Cannot confirm active warranty registration.

Warranty responses are based only on the configured NovaCare warranty policy and user-provided information.

---

# 2. No Case Creation

The assistant cannot create actual support tickets or service requests.

Limitations:

- Cannot submit warranty claims.
- Cannot create repair cases.
- Cannot generate official service requests.
- Cannot assign case numbers.

The assistant can only prepare a structured support summary for human review.

---

# 3. No Live Repair Status Tracking

The assistant does not have access to repair management systems.

Limitations:

- Cannot check repair progress.
- Cannot provide technician updates.
- Cannot provide estimated completion dates.
- Cannot retrieve previous service history.

---

# 4. Limited Product Coverage

The assistant supports only configured NovaRetail products.

Supported products:

- Lenovo ThinkPad E14 Gen 5 laptop.
- HP LaserJet Pro MFP M428-M429 printer.
- Bundled charger.
- Bundled power cable.

Limitations:

- Unsupported models cannot receive model-specific troubleshooting.
- Product information outside configured knowledge sources may not be available.

---

# 5. No Final Warranty Decisions

The assistant provides only preliminary warranty assessment.

Limitations:

- Cannot approve warranty claims.
- Cannot reject warranty claims.
- Cannot guarantee repair or replacement.
- Cannot make legal or commercial decisions.

Final decisions must be completed by an authorised NovaRetail representative.

---

# 6. No Emergency Service Integration

The assistant can identify safety risks and provide safety guidance but does not integrate with emergency services.

Limitations:

- Cannot call emergency services.
- Cannot notify emergency responders.
- Cannot remotely disable unsafe devices.

For immediate danger situations, customers must contact appropriate emergency services.

---

# 7. No Inventory Visibility

The assistant does not connect to inventory or supply systems.

Limitations:

- Cannot check product availability.
- Cannot reserve replacement units.
- Cannot provide stock information.
- Cannot estimate replacement delivery times.

---

# 8. No Remote Device Access

The assistant does not perform remote diagnostics.

Limitations:

- Cannot access customer devices.
- Cannot view device screens.
- Cannot execute commands remotely.
- Cannot inspect hardware physically.

---

# 9. Knowledge Source Dependency

The quality of responses depends on configured knowledge sources.

Limitations:

- Missing documentation may result in unavailable answers.
- Conflicting information requires source precedence rules.
- Unsupported technical details are not generated.

---

# 10. Data Privacy Limitations

The assistant does not collect unnecessary personal information.

Customers should not provide:

- Passwords.
- Banking information.
- Encryption keys.
- Private files.
- Confidential business data.

Only information required for support assessment should be shared.
