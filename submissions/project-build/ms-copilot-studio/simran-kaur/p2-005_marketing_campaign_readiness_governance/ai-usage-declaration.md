# AI Usage Declaration

AI assistance was used during development of the P2-005 project for:
- understanding the supplied project requirements;
- planning the multi-agent architecture;
- drafting implementation documentation;
- structuring test cases and evidence requirements;
- explaining Microsoft Copilot Studio implementation steps;
- reviewing orchestration and governance logic.

The final implementation, configuration, testing, evidence, and submission artifacts must be validated by the participant against the supplied PRD, datasets, policies, and actual Copilot Studio behavior.

AI assistance must not be treated as a substitute for source-of-truth policy interpretation. The supplied NovaSphere governance policy and brand/content guidelines remain authoritative for their respective rules.
