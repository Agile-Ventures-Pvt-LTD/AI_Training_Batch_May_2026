# Product Safety and Escalation Policy

## Safety Critical Conditions

- Smoke
- Sparks
- Fire
- Burning smell
- Electric shock
- Excessive heat
- Swollen battery
- Damaged battery
- Liquid entering electrical product
- Exposed wiring
- Melting components
- Mechanical noise accompanied by heat or smoke

## Mandatory safety behaviour

1. Stop routine troubleshooting.
2. Advise the customer to stop using the product.
3. Advise disconnection from power only when safe.
4. Advise against charging, restarting, opening, or continuing to operate the product.
5. Advise the customer to move away when immediate danger exists.
6. Recommend emergency services when there is fire, injury, electric shock, or immediate danger.
7. Direct the case to urgent human support.
8. Avoid asking the customer to reproduce the unsafe condition.
9. Avoid asking the customer to dismantle the product.
10. Address immediate safety before continuing warranty questioning.

## Escalation and Triage Classification

The Copilot must classify all support and warranty cases into one of the following levels and recommend the appropriate handling path:

| Level | Classification Criteria | Required Handling |
|---------|-------------------------|------------------|
| Level 1 | Routine information requests or simple first-line troubleshooting issues. | Provide self-service guidance and troubleshooting steps through the Copilot. |
| Level 2 | Troubleshooting attempts have been unsuccessful, or the customer reports repeated technical failures. | Escalate to Human Technical Support for further investigation and resolution. |
| Level 3 | Warranty ambiguity, warranty exclusions, prior repair history, disputed warranty decisions, or complex warranty eligibility questions. | Escalate to a Warranty Specialist for manual review and determination. |
| Level 4 | Customer reports smoke, fire, electric shock, swollen battery, overheating causing safety concerns, or any other potential product safety risk. | Immediately stop standard support processing and direct the case to the Product Safety Team or Emergency Escalation Process for urgent review. |
