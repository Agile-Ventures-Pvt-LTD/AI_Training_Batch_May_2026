# AI Usage

## AI Tools Used

- **Microsoft Copilot Studio** – Used to build and configure the NovaCare Product Support Assistant.
- **Generative AI / Copilot** – Used to assist with topic creation, instructions, conversation flows, and grounded responses.
- **ChatGPT** – Used for drafting documentation, structuring flows, refining prompts, and implementation guidance.

## AI-Assisted Activities

AI assistance was used for:

- Drafting agent instructions and descriptions.
- Designing troubleshooting and warranty topic flows.
- Suggesting trigger phrases and conversation starters.
- Structuring safety, escalation, and warranty logic.
- Drafting project documentation and test guidance.

## Generated Content

AI-generated content included initial drafts of agent instructions, topic structures, messages, documentation, and conditional-flow suggestions. Generated content was reviewed and adapted before being used in the final solution.

## Validation and Corrections

AI-generated outputs were manually checked against the project requirements, NovaRetail policies, product support scope, safety rules, and official Lenovo and HP documentation.

Incorrect, incomplete, or unsuitable outputs were corrected manually before final implementation.

## Manual Work

Manual work included:

- Configuring the agent in Copilot Studio.
- Uploading and configuring knowledge sources.
- Creating and modifying topics and subtopics.
- Creating variables, conditions, branches, and redirections.
- Implementing safety and warranty decision logic.
- Testing conversation flows and correcting errors.
- Reviewing knowledge grounding and final responses.
- Preparing project evidence and documentation.

## Understanding Confirmation

All AI-assisted content was reviewed and understood before inclusion in the project. The final implementation decisions, validation, testing, corrections, and submission remain the participant's responsibility.

No passwords, API keys, access tokens, or other secrets are included in the project documentation.
