# NovaCare Limited Warranty Policy

**Policy Version:** 1.0

**Effective Date:** 1 July 2026

**Company:** NovaRetail Technologies Pvt. Ltd.

---

# Purpose

This policy defines the preliminary warranty guidance that may be provided by the NovaCare Product Support & Warranty Assistant for supported NovaRetail products. The chatbot uses this policy to perform an initial assessment only. Final warranty decisions are always made by authorized NovaRetail representatives.

---

# Supported Products

The NovaCare Limited Warranty applies only to eligible products purchased directly from NovaRetail Technologies Pvt. Ltd.

Supported products include:

- Lenovo ThinkPad E14 Gen 5
- HP LaserJet Pro MFP M428–M429
- Bundled laptop battery
- Bundled accessories supplied with supported products

---

# Standard Warranty Coverage

The standard warranty period for eligible laptops and printers is **12 months** from the original invoice date.

The warranty covers manufacturing defects that occur during normal intended use.

---

# Battery and Accessory Coverage

Bundled laptop batteries are covered for **6 months** from the original invoice date.

Bundled accessories, including chargers and printer power cables supplied with the product, are also covered for **6 months**.

---

# Consumables

Printer consumables are **not covered** under the standard warranty.

Examples include:

- Toner cartridges
- Paper
- Ink
- Other consumable supplies

Damage caused by unsupported consumables requires review by a human warranty specialist.

---

# Required Documents

Customers should normally provide:

- Original purchase invoice
- Product serial number
- Product model information

The chatbot may explain that missing documents can delay or prevent preliminary warranty assessment.

---

# Dead-on-Arrival (DOA) Assessment

Products reported within **7 calendar days** of delivery may qualify for Dead-on-Arrival assessment if:

- Purchased directly from NovaRetail
- A hardware defect prevents normal intended use
- No accidental damage is present
- No liquid damage is present

A potential DOA assessment **does not guarantee replacement**. Final decisions require technical verification and approval.

---

# Covered Conditions

Examples of issues that may be covered include:

- Manufacturing defects
- Internal hardware failures
- Factory component failures
- Defects occurring during normal intended use

Coverage is subject to policy verification and human review.

---

# Warranty Exclusions

The standard warranty does not normally cover:

- Accidental damage
- Liquid damage
- Fire damage
- External electrical surges
- Misuse
- Negligence
- Improper storage
- Unauthorized repair
- Unauthorized modification
- Incompatible accessories
- Cosmetic damage that does not affect functionality
- Normal wear and tear
- Software configuration problems
- Malware or viruses
- Data loss
- Consumable items

The chatbot must identify applicable exclusions but must not issue a final rejection.

---

# Customer Responsibilities

Customers are responsible for:

- Backing up important data before service
- Completing basic approved troubleshooting where applicable
- Providing accurate purchase information
- Providing invoice details when available

---

# Privacy Requirements

The chatbot must never request:

- Passwords
- Banking information
- Encryption keys
- Payment card information
- Unrelated personal files

Only information required for preliminary troubleshooting or warranty assessment should be collected.

---

# Preliminary Warranty Guidance

The chatbot may classify a case as:

- Potentially Covered
- Potential Dead-on-Arrival Assessment
- Potentially Excluded
- Outside Standard Coverage
- Human Review Required
- Safety-Critical Escalation
- Insufficient Information

These classifications are preliminary and do not constitute a warranty decision.

---

# Human Review

Cases requiring human review include:

- Conflicting evidence
- Multiple previous repairs
- Customer disputes
- Unclear damage causes
- Unsupported product models
- Missing critical information

---

# Decision Boundary

The NovaCare Product Support & Warranty Assistant must never:

- Approve warranty claims
- Reject warranty claims
- Guarantee repair
- Guarantee replacement
- Confirm refunds
- Submit warranty requests
- Claim access to live warranty systems

Final decisions remain the responsibility of authorized NovaRetail personnel.
