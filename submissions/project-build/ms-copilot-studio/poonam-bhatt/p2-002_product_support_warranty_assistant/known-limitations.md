# Known Limitations

# P2-002 Product Support and Warranty Assistant

---

# 1. Overview

This document describes the known limitations and boundaries of the Product Support and Warranty Assistant.

The purpose of documenting limitations is to clearly define:

- What the assistant can do
- What the assistant cannot do
- Where human support is required
- Future improvement opportunities

---

# 2. Product Support Limitations

## 2.1 Supported Product Scope Limitation

The assistant provides model-specific troubleshooting only for supported products available in the knowledge base.

Currently supported scope:

- Lenovo supported laptops
- HP supported printers

For unsupported products:

- The assistant cannot provide model-specific troubleshooting steps.
- The customer is guided toward general assistance or escalation.

---

## 2.2 Limited Hardware Diagnosis

The assistant provides first-line troubleshooting guidance only.

The assistant cannot:

- Physically inspect devices
- Confirm hardware failure
- Perform component-level diagnosis
- Replace professional technician evaluation

Cases requiring physical inspection are routed for human support.

---

## 2.3 No Remote Access Capability

The assistant cannot:

- Access customer devices remotely
- Control customer systems
- View device screens
- Modify device settings directly
- Execute repair actions

Customers must perform suggested steps manually.

---

## 2.4 No Repair Completion Confirmation

The assistant cannot claim:

- Device repair completion
- Hardware replacement completion
- Service completion

It can only provide troubleshooting guidance and recommended next actions.

---

# 3. Safety Limitations

## 3.1 Safety Assessment Dependency

Safety classification depends on information provided by the customer.

If the customer does not disclose:

- Smoke
- Sparks
- Burning smell
- Liquid exposure
- Electric shock
- Excessive heat

the assistant may not detect the risk.

---

## 3.2 No Physical Safety Verification

The assistant cannot independently verify:

- Device temperature
- Battery condition
- Electrical hazards
- Physical damage

Customer-provided information is required.

---

## 3.3 Safety Override

Safety concerns always override troubleshooting.

When a safety-critical condition is detected:

- Normal troubleshooting stops.
- Escalation is recommended.
- The assistant does not ask customers to reproduce dangerous conditions.

---

# 4. Warranty Assessment Limitations

## 4.1 Preliminary Assessment Only

The warranty topic provides a preliminary assessment.

The assistant cannot:

- Approve warranty claims
- Reject warranty claims finally
- Guarantee repair
- Guarantee replacement

Final decisions require authorized human review.

---

## 4.2 No Live Warranty Database Access

The assistant does not have access to:

- Real-time warranty systems
- Customer purchase databases
- Service center records

Warranty evaluation is based only on information provided by the customer and configured policies.

---

## 4.3 Missing Information Dependency

Warranty classification may be limited when information is missing.

Examples:

- Missing purchase date
- Missing invoice information
- Missing product model
- Missing serial availability

In such cases, the assistant requests additional information or recommends review.

---

# 5. Knowledge Source Limitations

## 5.1 Knowledge Dependency

The quality of responses depends on available knowledge sources.

If required information is not available:

- The assistant may provide limited guidance.
- The case may require escalation.

---

## 5.2 Knowledge Update Requirement

New products, models, policies, or troubleshooting procedures require knowledge base updates before the assistant can provide accurate guidance.

---

# 6. Conversation Limitations

## 6.1 Customer Input Dependency

The assistant depends on customers providing accurate information.

Incorrect information may affect:

- Product validation
- Safety classification
- Warranty assessment
- Recommended next steps

---

## 6.2 Complex Issue Handling

Highly complex cases may require human expertise.

Examples:

- Multiple hardware failures
- Repeated unresolved failures
- Advanced diagnostics
- Legal warranty disputes

---

# 7. Current Automation Limitations

The assistant currently does not:

- Create service tickets automatically
- Schedule technician visits
- Process warranty claims directly
- Access customer account information
- Perform payments or service transactions

---

# 9. Conclusion

The Product Support and Warranty Assistant provides safe and structured customer assistance while maintaining clear boundaries.

The assistant focuses on:

- Safe troubleshooting
- Preliminary warranty assessment
- Knowledge-grounded responses
- Appropriate escalation

Human support remains essential for physical inspections, final warranty decisions, and complex technical cases.
