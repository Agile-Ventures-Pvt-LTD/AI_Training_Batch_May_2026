# AI Usage Declaration

We declare that AI assistants (including ChatGPT, Antigravity, and Copilot) were utilized constructively to support the planning, structuring, and documentation of this project.

## 🌟 Positive AI Contributions

AI tools were leveraged in a collaborative manner to enhance the quality, structure, and design of the campaign readiness governance solution:

1.  **Orchestration Design:** Assisted in planning the logical flow of the parallel fan-out/fan-in and selective reassessment patterns to ensure child specialist scopes remain clear and non-overlapping.
2.  **Prompt Engineering:** Assisted in drafting and structuring the custom instructions (prompts) for the child specialist agents.
3.  **Formula Logic Planning:** Assisted in verifying the logical flow of conditional Power Fx expressions (such as date comparisons, budget variance calculations, and regional routing thresholds).
4.  **Documentation Structure:** Guided the structure and organization of these markdown documentation templates to ensure comprehensive alignment with the requirements.
