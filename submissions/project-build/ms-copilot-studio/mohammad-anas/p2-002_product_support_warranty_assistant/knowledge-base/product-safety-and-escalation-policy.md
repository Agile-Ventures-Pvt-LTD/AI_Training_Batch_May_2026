# NovaRetail Product Safety and Escalation Policy

### Safety-Critical Conditions (Immediate Trigger Indicators)
If a customer reports or mentions any of the following conditions, the product is classified as a safety-critical hazard:
* Smoke or burning smell
* Sparks or visible electrical arcing
* Fire
* Electric shock from device, charger, or cable
* Excessive heat (device too hot to touch safely)
* Swollen, bulging, or physically damaged battery
* Liquid entering or leaking from an electrical product
* Exposed wiring or frayed cables
* Melting plastic or hardware components
* Unusual mechanical noise accompanied by smoke or heat

### Mandatory Safety Behavior
When a safety-critical condition is identified, the support assistant must strictly execute the following ten behavioral rules:
1. Stop routine troubleshooting immediately.
2. Advise the customer to stop using the product.
3. Advise disconnection from power only when safe to touch the plug or outlet.
4. Advise against charging, restarting, opening, or continuing to operate the product.
5. Advise the customer to move away when immediate danger exists.
6. Recommend emergency services when there is fire, injury, electric shock, or immediate danger.
7. Direct the case to urgent human support.
8. Avoid asking the customer to reproduce the unsafe condition.
9. Avoid asking the customer to dismantle the product.
10. Address immediate safety before continuing warranty questioning.

### Support Case Escalation Matrix

| Level | Classification | Required Handling & Routing |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Level 1** | Routine information or simple first-line troubleshooting | Self-service automated guidance via chatbot knowledge base. |
| **Level 2** | Troubleshooting unsuccessful or repeated technical failure | Route to Human Technical-Support review. |
| **Level 3** | Warranty ambiguity, exclusion, previous repair, or dispute | Route to Human Warranty Specialist review. |
| **Level 4** | Smoke, fire, electric shock, swollen battery, or safety risk | Immediate safety guidance, cease questioning, and urgent escalation. |
