# NovaCare Limited Warranty Policy

* **Policy version:** 1.0
* **Effective date:** 1 July 2026
* **Company:** NovaRetail Technologies Pvt. Ltd.

### Coverage & Eligibility Rules
1. NovaCare warranty guidance applies only to eligible products purchased directly from NovaRetail Technologies.
2. The standard product warranty is 12 months from the original invoice date.
3. A valid purchase invoice is normally required for warranty validation.
4. The product serial number must correspond to the product and invoice.
5. Products reported within seven calendar days of delivery may qualify for dead-on-arrival assessment when a hardware defect prevents normal intended use.
6. Dead-on-arrival assessment does not automatically guarantee replacement.
7. Replacement requires technical verification and approval by an authorised representative.
8. Standard warranty coverage applies to manufacturing defects occurring during normal intended use.

### Warranty Exclusions
9. Standard warranty does not normally cover accidental damage, liquid damage, fire damage, external electrical surges, misuse, negligence, improper storage, unauthorised repair, unauthorised modification, incompatible accessories, non-functional cosmetic damage, normal wear and tear, software configuration problems, viruses or malware, data loss, or consumable items.
10. Laptop batteries and bundled accessories are covered for six months from the original invoice date.
11. Printer toner, paper, and other consumables are not covered by the standard product warranty.
12. Damage potentially caused by unsupported consumables requires human review.

### Customer Responsibilities & Service Limitations
13. Warranty service does not include data backup, recovery, restoration, or migration.
14. Customers are responsible for backing up data before submitting a device for service.
15. Basic approved troubleshooting must normally be completed before repair escalation.
16. Customers must not be asked to share passwords, encryption keys, banking information, or unrelated personal files.

### Preliminary Guidance & Human Escalation Boundaries
17. Warranty guidance generated by the chatbot is preliminary.
18. Final warranty approval, rejection, repair, replacement, or commercial settlement must be completed by an authorised human representative.
19. The chatbot must not state that a repair, replacement, or warranty request has been submitted unless an integrated system successfully performs the action.
20. Cases involving unclear damage causes, conflicting evidence, multiple previous repairs, or customer disputes must be escalated for human review.
