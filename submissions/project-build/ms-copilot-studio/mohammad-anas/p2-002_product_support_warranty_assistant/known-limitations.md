# Known Limitations & System Boundaries

1. **No Live Database Lookup:** The chatbot is a standalone guidance tool. It does not connect to live ERP databases, customer order histories, payment gateways, or live repair tracking systems.
2. **No Automated Case Creation:** While the bot creates a formatted *Support Case Summary*, it cannot automatically book a technician, reserve replacement inventory, or generate a live ticketing system ticket number. Customers must submit the summary manually through their account portal.
3. **Preliminary Guidance Only:** All warranty eligibility classifications are strictly preliminary. The chatbot cannot issue legally binding approvals, final claim rejections, or commercial refunds.
4. **Limited Hardware Scope:** Automated troubleshooting only covers Lenovo ThinkPad E14/E16 laptops, HP LaserJet Pro M428-M429 printers, and bundled power accessories. Other brands are routed to Level 3 Human Support.
5. **No Emergency Service Dispatch:** In safety-critical events (smoke, fire, shock), the bot gives urgent disconnection advice but cannot directly dial 112 or dispatch physical security to the customer.
