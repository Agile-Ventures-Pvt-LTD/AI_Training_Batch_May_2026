## Known Limitations

- Supports only the configured products:
  - Lenovo ThinkPad E14 Gen 5
  - Lenovo ThinkPad E16 Gen 1
  - HP LaserJet Pro MFP M428-M429
- Provides only **preliminary** warranty guidance.
- Cannot approve or reject warranty claims.
- Cannot submit repair, replacement, or service requests.
- Does not provide live warranty verification.
- Does not provide live repair status, inventory, or order tracking.
- Answers are limited to the configured knowledge sources.
- Unsupported products receive only general guidance and escalation recommendations.

## Opening Instructions

1. Open the **Published URL** in a supported web browser.
2. Sign in if authentication is required.
3. Start a new conversation with the chatbot.
4. Ask questions related to:
   - Lenovo ThinkPad E14 Gen 5 or E16 Gen 1 laptops
   - HP LaserJet Pro MFP M428-M429 printers
   - Product troubleshooting
   - Warranty eligibility
   - Product safety
5. Follow the chatbot prompts to complete troubleshooting or warranty assessment.
6. Use the provided test cases to verify chatbot behavior.
7. Confirm that responses are grounded in the configured knowledge sources and comply with NovaRetail policies.
