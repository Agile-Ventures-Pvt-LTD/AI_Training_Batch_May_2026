# Known Limitations

The **NovaRetail Product Support & Warranty Assistant** is designed to provide safe, policy-compliant, first-line product support and preliminary warranty guidance. The following limitations apply to the current implementation.

---

## No Live Warranty Lookup

The chatbot does not have access to a live NovaRetail or manufacturer warranty database.

It cannot:

- Verify warranty status in real time.
- Validate warranty using a serial number.
- Confirm warranty registration.
- Check warranty expiration directly from manufacturer systems.

Warranty guidance is based solely on customer-provided information and the configured NovaCare Warranty Policy.

---

## No Case Creation

The chatbot cannot create support cases or service requests.

It cannot:

- Register warranty claims.
- Submit repair requests.
- Create replacement requests.
- Open technical support tickets.
- Schedule service appointments.

Any recommendation for repair or warranty review is informational only and requires human follow-up.

---

## No Live Repair Status

The chatbot has no integration with repair management systems.

It cannot:

- Check repair progress.
- Track repair completion.
- Provide technician updates.
- Display service center status.
- Estimate repair timelines.

Customers must contact NovaRetail support for live repair information.

---

## Limited Product Coverage

The chatbot supports only the configured product portfolio:

- Lenovo ThinkPad E14 Gen 5
- Lenovo ThinkPad E16 Gen 1
- HP LaserJet Pro MFP M428-M429
- Bundled laptop batteries
- Bundled laptop accessories
- Bundled printer power cables

Products outside this scope receive only general guidance and appropriate escalation recommendations.

---

## No Final Warranty Decisions

The chatbot performs only a preliminary warranty assessment.

It cannot:

- Approve warranty claims.
- Reject warranty claims.
- Guarantee repairs.
- Guarantee replacements.
- Authorize refunds.
- Make legal or commercial decisions.

Final decisions are made only by authorized NovaRetail representatives.

---

## Summary

The chatbot is intended to provide **safe, grounded, and policy-compliant first-line assistance**. Whenever a request requires live enterprise data, commercial decisions, unsupported products, or human judgment, the chatbot will clearly communicate its limitations and recommend the appropriate escalation path.
