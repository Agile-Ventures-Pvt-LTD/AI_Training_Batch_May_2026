# Known Limitations

## Overview

The NovaRetail Product Support & Warranty Assistant is designed to provide knowledge-grounded troubleshooting and preliminary warranty guidance for a limited set of supported products. While it offers accurate assistance within its defined scope, there are several functional and technical limitations.

---

# Product Scope Limitations

The chatbot supports only the following products:

- Lenovo ThinkPad E14 Gen 5
- HP LaserJet Pro MFP M428-M429
- Bundled Lenovo Charger
- Bundled Lenovo Power Cable

Products outside this list are not supported for model-specific troubleshooting or warranty guidance.

---

# Warranty Limitations

The chatbot provides **preliminary warranty guidance only**.

It cannot:

- Approve warranty claims.
- Reject warranty claims.
- Authorize repairs or replacements.
- Verify warranty status using customer records.
- Confirm proof of purchase.
- Override company warranty policies.

Final warranty decisions are made by authorised NovaRetail representatives.

---

# Technical Support Limitations

The chatbot can provide guided troubleshooting using official documentation but cannot:

- Diagnose hardware faults with certainty.
- Perform remote diagnostics.
- Access customer devices.
- Install drivers or software.
- Update firmware automatically.
- Execute commands on customer systems.
- Verify whether troubleshooting steps were physically completed.

---

# System Integration Limitations

The chatbot is not connected to enterprise systems and therefore cannot:

- Create support tickets.
- Book repair appointments.
- Assign technicians.
- Check repair status.
- Reserve replacement devices.
- Access inventory information.
- Retrieve customer account details.
- Access CRM or ERP systems.

---

# Knowledge Source Limitations

Responses are generated only from the configured knowledge sources.

The chatbot does not:

- Search the public internet.
- Use unofficial forums or blogs.
- Use social media content.
- Generate unsupported technical guidance.
- Answer questions outside the available documentation.

If the required information is unavailable, the chatbot recommends contacting human support.

---

# Safety Limitations

The chatbot can identify potential safety concerns based on customer responses but cannot:

- Detect hazards directly.
- Confirm the physical condition of a device.
- Replace professional safety advice.
- Assess environmental risks in real time.

Customers remain responsible for following safety instructions and contacting authorised support when required.

---

# Conversation Limitations

The chatbot may require additional information when:

- The product model is incomplete.
- The issue description is unclear.
- Warranty details are missing.
- Multiple issues are reported in the same conversation.

The chatbot relies on user-provided information and cannot independently verify its accuracy.

---

# AI Limitations

As an AI assistant, the chatbot:

- Relies on configured knowledge sources.
- May ask follow-up questions to clarify user intent.
- Cannot infer information that is not provided.
- Will avoid making assumptions when information is unavailable.
- May recommend escalation instead of providing uncertain guidance.

---

# Performance Limitations

Response quality depends on:

- The completeness of user input.
- The quality of the configured knowledge sources.
- Successful retrieval of relevant documentation.
- Availability of Microsoft Copilot Studio services.

Response times may vary depending on system load and knowledge retrieval.

---

# Security and Privacy Limitations

The chatbot does not:

- Store passwords or authentication credentials.
- Process payment information.
- Request government-issued identification.
- Retain sensitive personal information beyond the active conversation.

Users should avoid sharing confidential or sensitive information unless explicitly required for support.

---

# Future Enhancements

Potential improvements for future versions include:

- Integration with CRM and ticketing systems.
- Live warranty verification.
- Repair status tracking.
- Appointment scheduling.
- Inventory availability checks.
- Support for additional Lenovo and HP products.
- Multi-language support.
- Voice interaction.
- Customer authentication.
- Analytics and reporting dashboard.

---

# Summary

The Product Support & Warranty Assistant is designed to provide safe, consistent, and knowledge-grounded assistance within a clearly defined scope. It complements human support by resolving common issues and preparing customers for further assistance but does not replace authorised technical support or warranty specialists for final decisions and service operations.
