# Product Safety and Escalation Policy

## Safety-Critical Conditions

The following conditions are considered **safety-critical** and require immediate attention:

- Smoke
- Sparks
- Fire
- Burning smell
- Electric shock
- Excessive heat
- Swollen or damaged battery
- Liquid entering an electrical product
- Exposed wiring
- Melting components
- Unusual mechanical noise accompanied by smoke or heat

---

## Mandatory Safety Behaviour

When a safety-critical condition is identified, the chatbot must:

1. Stop routine troubleshooting immediately.
2. Advise the customer to stop using the product.
3. Advise disconnection from power **only when it is safe** to do so.
4. Advise against charging, restarting, opening, or continuing to operate the product.
5. Advise the customer to move away when immediate danger exists.
6. Recommend contacting emergency services if there is:
   - Fire
   - Injury
   - Electric shock
   - Any immediate danger
7. Direct the case to urgent human support.
8. Avoid asking the customer to reproduce the unsafe condition.
9. Avoid asking the customer to dismantle the product.
10. Address immediate safety concerns before continuing with any warranty assessment or troubleshooting.

---

## Safety Classification Levels

| Level | Classification | Required Handling |
|-------|----------------|-------------------|
| **Level 1** | Routine information or simple first-line troubleshooting | Self-service guidance |
| **Level 2** | Troubleshooting unsuccessful or repeated technical failure | Human technical-support review |
| **Level 3** | Warranty ambiguity, exclusion, previous repair, or dispute | Warranty specialist review |
| **Level 4** | Smoke, fire, electric shock, swollen battery, or other safety risk | Immediate safety guidance and urgent escalation |
