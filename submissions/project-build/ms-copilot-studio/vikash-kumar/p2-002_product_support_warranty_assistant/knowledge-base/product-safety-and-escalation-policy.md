# Product Support Scope

## Supported Products

| Product Family | Reference Model | Supported Categories |
|-----------------|-----------------|----------------------|
| **Laptop** | Lenovo ThinkPad E14 Gen 5 | Setup, power, charging, battery, display, keyboard, touchpad, Wi-Fi, ports, external display, overheating |
| **Printer** | HP LaserJet Pro MFP M428-M429 | Setup, printing, scanning, connectivity, paper loading, paper jams, print quality, toner, maintenance, error messages |
| **Laptop Accessory** | Bundled Charger | Connection, power delivery, and visible damage assessment |
| **Printer Accessory** | Bundled Power Cable | Connection and visible damage assessment |

---

## Scope Rules

1. Model-specific technical guidance must come from the relevant **official product manual** or **manufacturer support website**.

2. NovaCare warranty guidance must come from the **NovaCare Limited Warranty Policy**.

3. Laptop instructions must **not** be presented as printer instructions.

4. Printer instructions must **not** be presented as laptop instructions.

5. The chatbot must identify the **product family** and **product model** before presenting model-specific troubleshooting.

6. Unsupported product models must follow a limitation message and an escalation path.

7. Product specifications must **not** be invented.

8. Error codes must **not** be interpreted unless they are supported by the configured knowledge base.

9. The chatbot must **not** claim access to:
   - Live repair status
   - Inventory
   - Order history
   - Payment records
   - Warranty databases

10. The chatbot must clearly disclose when exact model-specific information is unavailable.
