P2-003 AUTONOMOUS EMAIL CONTENT REQUIREMENTS

These are business content patterns, not agent prompts. Participants must implement the required communication behaviour through Copilot Studio instructions and Outlook connector tools.

1. LEAD RECEIPT ACKNOWLEDGEMENT
Purpose: Confirm receipt of a valid Hot or Qualified lead.
Required content:
- Thank the sender.
- Confirm the business requirement and product interest received.
- State that a sales representative will review the inquiry.
- Avoid prices, commitments, promises, or qualification scores.
- Provide the generated Lead ID.

2. MISSING INFORMATION REQUEST
Purpose: Obtain only information required for qualification.
Required content:
- Confirm receipt.
- List the specific missing fields.
- Explain that the information is needed to route the inquiry correctly.
- Avoid requesting passwords, payment information, government identifiers, or confidential technical material.

3. INTERNAL HOT LEAD ALERT
Purpose: Notify the assigned owner and sales operations.
Required content:
- Lead ID, company, contact, product, score, classification, decision confidence, timeline, budget, risk flags, assigned owner.
- Path or link to the Word qualification report.
- Required response priority.

4. INTERNAL QUALIFIED LEAD NOTIFICATION
Purpose: Notify the assigned owner.
Required content:
- Lead summary, score, classification, missing information, recommended next action, report path.

5. HUMAN REVIEW ALERT
Purpose: Route an exception without sending an external qualification result.
Required content:
- Exception reason, confidence, missing or conflicting information, actions completed, action that was withheld.

6. NURTURE ACKNOWLEDGEMENT
Purpose: Confirm receipt without overcommitting.
Required content:
- Thank the sender.
- Confirm the area of interest.
- State that the inquiry will be retained for follow-up.
- Request one or two missing items only when relevant.

7. NON-SALES / SUPPORT ROUTING
Purpose: Prevent sales processing of a support, recruitment, or academic request.
Required content:
- Do not reveal internal classification.
- Do not create a qualification report.
- Route internally only when an approved internal recipient is configured.
