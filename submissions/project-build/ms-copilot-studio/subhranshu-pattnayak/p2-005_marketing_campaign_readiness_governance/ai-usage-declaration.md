## Project

## Purpose of AI Usage

Artificial Intelligence was used as a development assistance tool during the design, implementation, testing, and documentation phases of this project.

The final solution architecture, business logic, orchestration flow, governance rules, specialist responsibilities, and implementation decisions were reviewed and validated by the project author.
