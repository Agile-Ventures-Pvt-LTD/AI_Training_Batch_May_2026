# Known Limitations

The current implementation has the following limitations:

## Product Coverage

Supports only:

- Lenovo ThinkPad E14 Gen 5
- HP LaserJet Pro MFP M428-M429

---

## Warranty

- Preliminary assessment only
- Cannot approve warranty claims
- Cannot reject warranty claims
- Cannot create warranty requests

---

## Integrations

No integration with:

- CRM
- Ticketing system
- Repair management
- Inventory
- Order history
- Live warranty database
- Live repair tracking

---

## Troubleshooting

- Limited to configured knowledge sources
- No remote diagnostics
- No remote access
- No firmware deployment

---

## Safety

Safety guidance is informational.

Emergency situations must be handled by emergency services or authorized human representatives.

---

## AI

Responses are grounded using configured knowledge sources.

When information is unavailable, the assistant may request clarification or recommend human support.
