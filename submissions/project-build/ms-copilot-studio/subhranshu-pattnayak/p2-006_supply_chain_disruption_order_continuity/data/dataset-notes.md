# Dataset Notes

## Overview

The solution uses a sample supply disruption workbook that simulates a supply continuity management process. The dataset provides the operational information required by the Supervisor, Specialists, Topics, and Reporting workflow.

---

## Data Sources

The workbook contains the following logical datasets:

- Disruption Requests
- SKU Master
- Inventory
- Purchase Orders
- Customer Orders
- Suppliers
- Alternate Suppliers
- Recovery Rules

---

## Purpose

The dataset supports:

- Disruption intake and validation
- Inventory impact assessment
- Alternate supplier evaluation
- Customer impact analysis
- Commercial impact analysis
- Recovery planning
- Approval determination
- Final reporting and communication

---

## Assumptions

- The workbook is used for demonstration and testing purposes.
- Sample records represent realistic supply disruption scenarios.
- Specialists use workbook data as the primary evidence source.
- Missing or incomplete records may result in Insufficient Evidence outcomes.

---

## Limitations

- Dataset size is intentionally small for testing.
- Does not represent real production volumes.
- Approval rules are simplified according to PRD requirements.
- Communication recipients are predefined test users.
