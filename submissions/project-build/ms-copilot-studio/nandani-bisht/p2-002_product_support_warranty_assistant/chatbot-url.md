## Opening and Testing Instructions

To access and interact with the chatbot for evaluation, please follow these steps:

1. **Open the URL:** Click on the published Copilot Studio Demo Website URL (or paste the URL provided in your evaluation email into your web browser).
2. **Initialize Conversation:** 
   - The chatbot will automatically load and present the welcome message.
   - Alternatively, type `Hello` or select one of the four conversation starters to initiate.
3. **Select a Conversation Starter:**
   - *“Help me set up my ThinkPad E14 laptop”*
   - *“How do I check my printer warranty?”*
   - *“What should I do if my charger shows physical damage?”*
   - *“My laptop battery is draining very fast”*
4. **Trigger Custom Topics:**
   - To trigger **Guided Product Troubleshooting**: Enter queries such as *"troubleshoot laptop displays"*, *"my printer is offline"*, or *"poor print quality support"*.
   - To trigger **Warranty Assessment**: Enter queries such as *"check warranty status"*, *"am I eligible for a warranty repair?"*, or *"how long is my laptop covered?"*.
5. **Safety Triage:**
   - Any mention of safety-critical indicators (e.g., *"smoke"*, *"sparks"*, *"burning smell"*) will automatically redirect to the Product Safety Assessment, stop routine troubleshooting, advise safety steps, and trigger a Level 4 escalation.

## Known Access Limitations

> [!WARNING]
> - **Session Expiration:** If the webchat window is left idle for more than 15 minutes, the session will expire. Refresh the page to start a new chat.
> - **Tenant Boundaries:** If tenant sharing policies are strictly enforced by your organization, ensure you are logged into the designated tenant account or accessing it via the allowed public demo URL.
