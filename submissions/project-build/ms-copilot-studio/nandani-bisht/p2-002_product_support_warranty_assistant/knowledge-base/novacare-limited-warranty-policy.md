# NovaCare Limited Warranty Policy

**Policy Version:** 1.0  
**Effective Date:** 1 July 2026  
**Company:** NovaRetail Technologies Pvt. Ltd.

## Warranty Eligibility

- NovaCare warranty applies only to eligible products purchased directly from NovaRetail Technologies.
- The standard warranty period is **12 months** from the original purchase invoice date.
- A valid purchase invoice is normally required for warranty verification.
- The product serial number should correspond with the purchased product and invoice.

## Dead-on-Arrival (DOA)

- Products reported within **7 calendar days** of delivery may qualify for Dead-on-Arrival (DOA) assessment if a hardware defect prevents normal operation.
- DOA assessment does not automatically guarantee replacement.
- Replacement requires technical verification and approval by an authorized NovaRetail representative.

## Standard Warranty Coverage

The standard warranty generally covers manufacturing defects occurring during normal intended use.

## Standard Warranty Exclusions

The warranty does not normally cover:

- Accidental damage
- Liquid damage
- Fire damage
- External electrical surges
- Misuse or negligence
- Improper storage
- Unauthorized repair
- Unauthorized modification
- Use of incompatible accessories
- Cosmetic damage that does not affect functionality
- Normal wear and tear
- Software configuration problems
- Malware or viruses
- Data loss
- Consumable items

## Battery and Accessories

- Laptop batteries are covered for **6 months**.
- Bundled accessories are covered for **6 months**.
- Printer toner, paper, and other consumables are **not covered** under the standard warranty.
- Damage caused by unsupported consumables requires human review.

## Customer Responsibilities

- Customers should back up their data before submitting devices for service.
- Warranty service does not include data backup, restoration, migration, or recovery.
- Basic approved troubleshooting should normally be completed before repair escalation.

## Privacy

The assistant must never request:

- Passwords
- Encryption keys
- Banking information
- Unrelated personal files

## Important Notice

- Warranty guidance provided by the chatbot is **preliminary only**.
- Final warranty approval, rejection, repair, replacement, or commercial settlement can only be made by an authorized NovaRetail representative.
- The chatbot must never claim that a repair request, warranty claim, or replacement request has been submitted unless an integrated backend system actually performs the action.
- Cases involving unclear damage, conflicting evidence, multiple previous repairs, or customer disputes must be escalated for human review.
